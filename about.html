Our story

One table, one scale, one promise.

Nkwen Traders started in 1998 with a single table at Nkwen Market, a bathroom scale for weighing rice, and a promise from our founder, Mama Agnes Ngwa: everyone pays the same price, whether they buy one cup of beans or a whole bag.

Back then the whole stock fit under one umbrella — a few bags of rice, a tin of palm oil, soap, salt, and matches. Word travelled faster than the goods did. Neighbours sent their children with a list and a few coins, and the list rarely changed: something to cook, something to wash with, something to light the stove.

"If a customer cannot trust the price, they cannot trust the food that comes from it." — Mama Agnes Ngwa, founder

Almost thirty years later, the table has grown into a proper stall with a full shelf of everyday staples, but the rule from day one has not moved: fair weight, fair price, no exceptions. This site is the newest addition — a way for regular customers to check what's in stock before making the walk to Nkwen Market.

We are still, at heart, a family business run by people who know most of our customers by name. The website changes how you find us. It does not change how we treat you.
